How we work

Four stages, with a review at the end of each one so there are no surprises.

  1. Discovery

    We learn your goals, users and budget, then agree on scope and timeline.

  2. Design

    Wireframes and clickable Figma prototypes you can test before any code is written.

  3. Development

    Two-week sprints with a working demo at the end of each one.

  4. QA & Launch

    Device and browser testing, store submission, and 30 days of free support.
